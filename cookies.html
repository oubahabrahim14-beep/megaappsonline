What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function, remember your preferences, and measure traffic.

How to Control Cookies

You can manage or disable cookies in your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

To opt out of Google personalized ads: Google Ad Settings · Your Online Choices (EU) · AboutAds (US)

GDPR / Consent

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, or another region requiring cookie consent, advertising cookies are only activated after you consent. You may withdraw consent at any time by clearing your cookies and adjusting browser settings.
